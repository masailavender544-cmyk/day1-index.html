About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture ideas quickly and stay organized. It is designed for students and professionals who need a lightweight note‑taking tool.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes in your browser.
  2. Type your notes directly into the editor.
  3. Save or export your notes when finished.

Features

  • Simple, distraction‑free interface
  • Autosave functionality
  • Export to text or PDF

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save current note
